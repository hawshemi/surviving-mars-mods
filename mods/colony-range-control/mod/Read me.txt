Colony Range Control

Adjust 17 colony ranges from 1x to 10x in Surviving Mars: Relaunched.

Version 1.1 (1.01-002). By hawshemi.

Install
Copy this folder into %APPDATA%\Surviving Mars Relaunched\Mods\LocalRangeExtender. Enable the mod in Mod Manager and restart the game. Use a separate test save.

Use
Choose Normal, 2x, 5x, 10x, or Custom in Mod Options. Normal is the default. Presets set every slider. Changing one slider selects Custom. Apply to update your colony.

Options
- Range preset
- Dome and station range
- Drone Hub range
- RC Commander range
- Rocket drone range
- Drone Hub Extender range
- Scrubber range
- Heater range
- Meteor laser range
- Extractor deposit range
- Sensor scanning reach
- Artificial Sun range
- Underground support range
- Safari attraction range
- Safari route length
- Forestation Plant range
- Mohole heat range
- Advanced Stirling heat range

Limits
Larger heater and scrubber ranges use more power.

Remove
Select Normal and apply in a loaded colony. Save, disable the mod in Mod Manager, and restart. Existing safari routes stay unchanged.
