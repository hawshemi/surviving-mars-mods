# Colony Capacity Control

![Colony Capacity Control for Surviving Mars: Relaunched](mod/preview.jpg)

Adjust 38 colony capacities from 1x to 10x in Surviving Mars: Relaunched.

Version 1.1 (`1.01-002`). By hawshemi.

## Install

Copy the contents of `mod` into `%APPDATA%\Surviving Mars Relaunched\Mods\ColonyCapacityControl`. Enable the mod in Mod Manager and restart the game. Use a separate test save.

## Use

Choose Normal, 2x, 5x, 10x, or Custom in Mod Options. Normal is the default. Presets set every slider. Changing one slider selects Custom. Apply to update your colony.

## Limits

Changes capacity without creating resources, drones, or shuttles. Native technology, sponsor, upgrade, and law modifiers still apply. More drones and shuttles can increase simulation load. Larger storage stacks may extend beyond their models.

## Remove

Select Normal and apply in a loaded colony. Wait until no reductions are pending. Unload cargo, drain excess storage, reduce fleets, or wait for visitors to leave as needed. Save, disable the mod in Mod Manager, and restart.
